Grantor is not published in BCPAO data; the Brevard Clerk of Court Official Records (search by OR Book/Page) lists grantor and grantee for each deed. Acreage is the summed GIS parcel-polygon area of every parcel on the deed, so price per acre reflects the whole conveyance. Multi-parcel deeds are mapped at the primary (largest vacant, addressed) parcel. Coordinates are parcel centroids from the BCPAO ArcGIS parcel layer, not geocoded (one brand-new split parcel, 27-36-23-00-522, is plotted from its legal description because BCPAO GIS has not drawn it yet). Zoning shown only where the parcel falls in unincorporated Brevard County zoning; city-zoned parcels are blank. This run covers deeds newly posted to BCPAO since the last Brevard update (data refreshed 11/20/2026), de-duplicated against the ELC database by OR Book/Page.
